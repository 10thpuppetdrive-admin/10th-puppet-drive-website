Our Story

A little chaos. A lot of heart. A dream that never disappeared. ❤️

Mobi & Torik were born in 1995 as original characters for radio comedy sketches. In 2002, original drawings were created with the dream of bringing them to life as a cartoon. That dream couldn't become a reality at the time, but the characters and their stories were never forgotten.

More than two decades later, technology opened a new door.

Today, 10th Puppet Drive is an independent creative production bringing these original characters to life. Our stories, humor, character designs, voice acting, sound design, and creative direction are human-led. We use AI as a supporting visual production tool.

Behind the Chaos

“For years, I worked in a completely different field, never imagining that Mobi & Torik would finally come to life. After 26 years, technology gave me the opportunity to realize a dream I once thought was out of reach — independently, without giving up ownership of my ideas and characters.”

At 10th Puppet Drive, we believe that laughter brings people together, friendship is stronger than chaos, and some dreams are worth holding on to.

Thank you for watching, sharing, and being part of our little puppet family. ❤️
